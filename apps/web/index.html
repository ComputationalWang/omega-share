How it works

  1. The extension finds the video

    Open a page with a video on it and click the omega-share button in your browser.

  2. Share it into a room

    Pick a room and press Share. Friends join with the room's link, no account needed.

  3. Sit together and watch

    Everyone sees the same moment. Take a seat, chat and wave.
