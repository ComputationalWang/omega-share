PageOne screen: height: 100dvh, no page scroll. A flex column: top bar 40 · video 16:9 · shelf 44 · room = the rest · composer 52 + safe-area inset. The footer, invite and report move into sheets.
Top barRoom name (ellipsis), people count (opens People), Host key (owners only, mustard rim = only you) and Leave.
Video + shelfUnchanged from set (k): full width, compact bezel and shelf. The video is never covered by a sheet or the keyboard.
RoomStill 1×, never scaled, dragged sideways. It now gets 270–460 px of height instead of a fixed 270, so the camera centres on the seats vertically too. The up-next chip sits top right.
Chat overlayThe last 3 lines float over a scrim at the foot of the room (settled cards, newest last), with a grab handle and an unread count. Tap or swipe up → the chat sheet. Lines older than 8 s fade to 2 (set k's strip timing); system lines stay in the log only.
ComposerDocked at the foot like a messaging app: emote key · message · share-a-link key · send. The share key is ui-icon-queue-add (aria "Share a video link"). Send is disabled until there is text.
Keyboard openTyping a message: the top bar, shelf and room fold away; the video stays, the chat log fills the space down to the composer, which rides on the keyboard (visualViewport / interactive-widget=resizes-content). Blur → the room comes back.
Chat sheetCovers the room, not the video. Full log, scrolls, newest at the foot; the composer stays. Close key or swipe down.
Share sheetThe existing Up next panel as a sheet: head "Up next n / N", "Play next now" for the host, the list, then the paste field and Add at the foot of the sheet, right above the keyboard. Opened by the share key (field focused) or the up-next chip (field not focused). Empty: "Nothing up next. Paste a video link to load a reel."
EmotesThe emote key opens set (i)'s picker as a tray above the composer, tail on the key. One tap sends and closes it.
HostHost key → host sheet: Who controls playback (Everyone / Only me), the invite link with Copy, "Arrange the room on a computer". Tapping a name tag → host options as an action sheet at the foot (Mute chat, Remove from room, then the confirm).
Focus orderleave · host · people · pause · seek · full screen · room · up next · chat log · emote · message · share · send. A sheet traps focus, Esc closes it, focus returns to its key.
