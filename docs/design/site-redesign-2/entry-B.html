How it works

A good evening, in three steps.

  1. Bring something good.

    Find a video on YouTube, Twitch or Vimeo. Or share a page with the extension.

  2. Leave the door open.

    Send your room link to a friend. They choose a name and come right in.

  3. Be here, together.

    Press play, chat a little, stay a while. Supported videos play in sync.
