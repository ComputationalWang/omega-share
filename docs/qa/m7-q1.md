### Known a11y bugs: [OME-701](/OME/issues/OME-701)

The a11y walk found six product bugs. Each one is a `test.fail` in `e2e/m7-a11y.e2e.ts`, so the suite stays green and a fix turns its test red until someone flips it. The rest of the walk fails on anything new. All six are filed as [OME-701](/OME/issues/OME-701):

1. After joining, focus falls to `<body>`.
2. Esc out of full screen with focus on the strip key: the key hides and focus falls to `<body>`.
3. Seats have no visible focus indicator.
4. `[data-testid=queue-url]` has no visible focus indicator: `.ui-input` swaps its border-image on focus but sets `outline: none`.
5. `[data-testid=report-note]` has no visible focus indicator: same `.ui-input` cause.
6. axe `listitem`: chat lines are `<li>`s directly inside the `role=log` `<div>`. This applies in the page, the strip and chat.html.
