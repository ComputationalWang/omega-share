# ADR 0060: The band fits the room, below 1× where it must; the phone's Fit fits the walls

**Status:** accepted (2026-11-01) · Lead · [OME-2148](/OME/issues/OME-2148) (design judge round 2 on OME-2122, QA evidence [OME-2146](/OME/issues/OME-2146#document-review) findings 1 and 3). Amends ADR 0059 and OME-1811's whole-number rule for the audience band.

**Context:** ADR 0059 gave the 1280×800 band half the room (266 px) at 1× and pointed it at the audience. From the back wall's tallest art (stage y 113) to the lowest name tag, every preset needs 377 to 442 stage px, so no preset fits at 1×. The empty standing spots by the door pulled the band down to y 538, which put the cinema's back-row heads under the TV, and in the Dank Dungeon and the Studio my own name tag went under the band's foot. On the phone, Fit fitted the 960×600 stage. The walls are 660 px of that width, so the room filled about 60 % of a 412 px phone.

## Decision

1. **The band fits the room** (`bandFit`, `apps/web/src/layout.ts`). It uses the largest whole scale up to `roomScale` that holds `BAND_WALL_TOP` (y 113) down to the lowest name tag foot, counting every seat, every standing spot and me. If none does, it uses the fraction that does, never under 0.5. At 1280×800 that is 0.60 to 0.70. The band's box is no wider than the room at that scale, centred, and its backdrop still bleeds to the column's edges (OME-1843). A change in my spot refits only when it changes the scale.
2. **Names and bubbles stay readable below 1×:** the band gets the phone's counter-scale (`labelScale`, ≥ 0.85 of their 1× size on screen, `.is-fit`).
3. **The phone's Fit fits the walls** (`ROOM_WALLS`, 660×532 stage px), not the stage. A window that holds them shows them centred (`panTo`). "Full width" is the room pane's width: the zoom keys keep their 52 px column beside it (OME-1302, keys over the room hid seats). With a video at 412×915 the pane is 360×358, so Fit is width-bound (360/660 < 358/532): the walls span the pane, and the 68 px left over sits evenly above and below the room (QA [OME-2156](/OME/issues/OME-2156) finding 2).

**Amendment (2026-11-02, [OME-2170](/OME/issues/OME-2170)):** the room's foot is the lowest name tag's or the floor's front corner (`FLOOR_FOOT`, y 540), whichever is lower. A band that holds the back wall's art to that foot centres on it. Before this, the north views of the penthouse and the studio fitted to their tags, which sit above the corner, so the front rows' furniture was cut (QA [OME-2168](/OME/issues/OME-2168#document-review) § 8 item 1). The walls' and partitions' tops above y 113 stay cropped as before.

**Amendment 2 (2026-11-02, [OME-2170](/OME/issues/OME-2170), CEO decision C):** at 1280×800 a hero TV leaves the band 0.60 to 0.70×, so avatars (41 to 47 px at 1×) are about 25 px. Readable avatars need the room at about 1×, 428 to 442 px of the column's 655, which a hero TV can't leave. So the wide layout gets **Room view**: a labelled toggle key (`room-view-key`, `aria-pressed`) in the band's top corner, shown only with a video, out of full screen and while the room is here. On, `wideLayout(…, roomView)` puts the TV at its ADR 0012 floor (356×200, Twitch 534×300) on the left with its shelf beside it, where the shelf's keys fit (else under it, as before), and the band takes the rest: 441 px at 1280×800, so every preset fits at 0.998× or more, in both swap views. Off is the hero layout, unchanged, and stays the default. The choice lives in the tab's memory only (no storage, no contract change). Switching re-boxes the TV, the shelf and the stage; it never rebuilds the player or the stage.

**Amendment 3 (2026-11-02, [OME-2195](/OME/issues/OME-2195), CEO decision after judge round 4, [OME-2061](/OME/issues/OME-2061)):** Room view is the **default from a 1100 px wide window**. Below that width, and on the phone, the video-first layout stays. The viewer's choice is kept on this device (`localStorage` `omega.roomView`, `on`/`off`; anything else counts as no choice) and beats the default either way. This reverses amendment 2's "off stays the default" and "tab memory only". The geometry stays amendment 2's: the shelf sits beside the TV where its keys fit, now only its keys wide (420 px × the page zoom, Twitch 560), flush to the TV, never stretched to the column's right edge. Docking the shelf under the TV was declined (CEO decision B): avatar size comes first; a dock that moves the reaction bar into the space right of the TV stays an optional design pass. At 1280×800 the real column is about 888×618 (the reaction bar and the footer sit under it). A docked shelf costs the band 52 px, about 34 px avatars instead of about 39. A shelf hung over the band's corner hides the tops of the left wall's posters and windows. The first-visit tour's chip also follows the TV's box (a ResizeObserver), because Room view re-boxes the TV after a video's screen swap.

## Consequences

- At 1× windows the band's pixel art is drawn below 1× (nearest-neighbour, like the phone's zoomed-out window). Whole-number scales stay wherever the band holds the span: 1280×1200 and 2× windows are unchanged.
- The TV keeps ADR 0059's size. The hero bound (43 %) is untouched.
- Rows of the room below the lowest name tag (the floor's front corner) can sit under the band's foot, and a wheel drag still reaches them.
- A fractional band scale gives the bubbles a fractional label scale, and float rounding can then leave a sub-pixel overlap that `floats.ts resolve()` never cleared (a frozen tab, QA OME-2156). Overlap under 1e-6 px counts as none (`SLACK`).
- Reversing: drop `bandFit` from `room-layout.ts`. The band is then ADR 0059's again. Room view goes with its key and `wideLayout`'s `roomView` branch.
